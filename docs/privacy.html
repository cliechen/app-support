# Privacy Policy Effective: November 17, 2026 This privacy policy applies to the following iOS applications developed by cliechen ("we", "our", or "us"): - TabCaddy (com.cliechen.TabCaddy) - CremaShuffle (com.cliechen.CremaShuffle) - HanVault (com.cliechen.HanVault) - DiceTrove (com.cliechen.DiceTrove) - Coin Flip (com.cliechen.CoinFlip) - Private Vault (com.cliechen.PrivateVault) - Animal Pop (com.cliechen.AnimalPop) - DayVault (com.cliechen.DayVault) - PopCritters (com.cliechen.PopCritters) ## Data Collection Except for the advertising services described below, our apps are designed to operate locally on your device: - No analytics or user-tracking accounts are created by the developer. - No personal information (such as name, email, or contacts) is requested or collected by the developer. - Core calculation, game logic, and vault data execute entirely on-device. ## Local Data Storage The following app data is stored locally on your device only, using iOS UserDefaults or the app's document directory: - TabCaddy: User preferences, tip percentages, custom diner names, and settlement drafts (stored locally in UserDefaults using reason CA92.1). - CremaShuffle: Best score, unlocked achievements, effect settings, music/SFX preferences, and tutorial status (stored locally in UserDefaults using reason CA92.1). - HanVault: Learning progress, SRS review records, bookmarked words, daily mission state, and study statistics (stored locally in UserDefaults using reason CA92.1). - DiceTrove: Roll history (stored locally in UserDefaults using reason CA92.1). - Coin Flip: Flip history and statistics (stored locally in UserDefaults using reason CA92.1). - Private Vault: Encrypted photos and passcode (stored locally, never transmitted). - Animal Pop: Game progress and level data (stored locally in UserDefaults using reason CA92.1). - DayVault: Calendar events, journal entries, custom event types, and period cycle data (stored locally in the document directory). - PopCritters: Game progress, level data, booster inventory, and user preferences (stored locally in UserDefaults using reason CA92.1). All locally stored data is removed when you delete the app from your device. We do not keep server-side copies of it. ## Advertising and Third-Party Services Our games are free to play and supported by advertising. Core gameplay and local data stay on your device; an internet connection is used only to load and display ads. **PopCritters** shows a single opted-in rewarded video when a booster runs out. There are no banners and no interstitials, and no ad is shown unless the player taps the "Watch Ad" button. Ads are requested only after Google's UMP consent tool has recorded the player's choice for their region; where consent is required, no ad is requested before that choice is made. PopCritters does not request App Tracking Transparency permission and does not use the IDFA, so ad requests are not personalized for advertising across other apps or websites. **TabCaddy** shows banner and occasional interstitial ads. TabCaddy asks for App Tracking Transparency permission before any tracking identifier is requested; if you decline, only non-personalized ads are shown. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process the following data when serving or measuring ads: - Advertising data — which ad was requested, shown, or tapped. - Device identifier — a device-level identifier used by Google to serve and measure ads. It is not linked to your identity. - Approximate (coarse) location — derived from the network connection, not from GPS permission. - Product interaction — ad-related actions inside the app. None of this data is linked to your identity, none of it is used to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies, and we never sell it. The developer does not receive personally identifiable information from the ads SDK. You can review or change your ad consent at any time inside the app: **Settings → Privacy & Ads → Ad Privacy Choices**. Where required, Google's UMP consent form is shown on first launch and can be reopened from that screen. For more information about how Google handles this data: - Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy - How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites - Google advertising technologies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads ## Encryption Private Vault uses AES-GCM encryption via Apple's CryptoKit to encrypt all photos stored in the vault. The encryption key is derived from your passcode and never leaves your device. No one — including us — can access your encrypted photos without your passcode. ## Security Private Vault protects access with Face ID / Touch ID and a passcode lock screen. DayVault also uses Face ID to lock the app and protect your private journal entries. The utility apps (CremaShuffle, HanVault, DiceTrove, Coin Flip, Private Vault, Animal Pop, DayVault) operate strictly offline with no external network transmission. ## Children's Privacy Our apps do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. All content is suitable for general audiences. The ads shown in PopCritters and TabCaddy are not directed at children, and neither app is listed in the Kids category. ## Third-Party Services and Data Sharing Apart from Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent tool in PopCritters and TabCaddy, none of our apps integrate third-party SDKs, analytics tracking, or external APIs. We do not sell or share personal data with third parties. ## Changes to This Policy We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with an updated effective date. ## Contact Us If you have any questions about this privacy policy or want to exercise a data-related request, please contact us at cliechen@gmail.com.
