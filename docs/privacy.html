Privacy Policy

  • TabCaddy (com.cliechen.TabCaddy)

Data Collection

Except for the advertising services described below, our apps are designed to operate locally on your device:

  • No analytics or user-tracking accounts are created by the developer.
  • No personal information (such as name, email, or contacts) is requested or collected by the developer.
  • Core calculation, game logic, and vault data execute entirely on-device.

Local Data Storage

The following app data is stored locally on your device only, using iOS UserDefaults or the app's document directory:

  • PopCritters: game progress, level unlocks and star ratings, coin balance, booster inventory, achievements, login streak, daily challenge, daily mission and Mystery Chest state, daily deal state, the selected theme, sound, music, haptics and accessibility preferences, an in-progress level session, the local ad-reward ledger (which rewarded offer was credited and when), and an in-app activity log of up to 500 recent gameplay and ad events (stored locally in UserDefaults using reason CA92.1).
  • TabCaddy: your preferences (tip presets, pre-tax default, Zen rounding, haptics and sound, settlement style), your Venmo username if you save one, venue and table labels, party size and diner names, the in-progress check draft, and a local history of up to 30 completed checks (venue, table label, totals, per-person amounts and the settlement text). All of it is stored locally in UserDefaults using reason CA92.1.
  • CremaShuffle: Best score, unlocked achievements, effect settings, music/SFX preferences, and tutorial status (stored locally in UserDefaults using reason CA92.1).
  • HanVault: Learning progress, SRS review records, bookmarked words, daily mission state, and study statistics (stored locally in UserDefaults using reason CA92.1).
  • DiceTrove: Roll history (stored locally in UserDefaults using reason CA92.1).
  • Coin Flip: Flip history and statistics (stored locally in UserDefaults using reason CA92.1).
  • Private Vault: Encrypted photos and passcode (stored locally, never transmitted).
  • Animal Pop: Game progress and level data (stored locally in UserDefaults using reason CA92.1).
  • DayVault: Calendar events, journal entries, custom event types, and period cycle data (stored locally in the document directory).

All locally stored data is removed when you delete the app from your device. We do not keep server-side copies of it.

Advertising and Third-Party Services

PopCritters and TabCaddy are free to use and supported by advertising. Core calculation and game logic, and all data you enter, stay on your device. TabCaddy uses the network in three cases only:

  • to request and display ads;
  • when you type a Venmo username in Settings, TabCaddy requests that public profile page (after you pause typing) to confirm the username exists;
  • when you tap a payment link, TabCaddy hands the recipient's username and the amount per person to Venmo or Cash App — together with your optional note when the link goes to Venmo — and those services then apply their own privacy policies.

TabCaddy never reads your Venmo credentials, contacts, or payment history.

PopCritters shows a single opted-in rewarded video for its "empty booster" offer, which appears in a level or in the Booster Backpack when you run out of a booster. There are no banners and no interstitials in this version, and no ad is shown unless you tap the "Watch Ad" button. Ads are requested only after Google's UMP consent tool reports that ads may be requested for your region (after you answer its consent form where one is required); where consent is required and not yet given, no ad is requested. PopCritters does not request App Tracking Transparency permission and does not use the IDFA, so Google cannot use it to track you across other apps or websites.

TabCaddy shows banner and occasional interstitial ads. TabCaddy asks for App Tracking Transparency permission before any tracking identifier is requested; if you decline, only non-personalized ads are shown.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process the following data when serving or measuring ads:

  • Advertising data — which ad was requested, shown, or tapped.
  • Device identifier — a device-level identifier used by Google to serve and measure ads. It is not linked to your identity.
  • Approximate (coarse) location — derived from the network connection, not from GPS permission.
  • Product interaction — ad-related actions inside the app.

This data is not linked to your name, email, or account: our apps have no account system. If you allow App Tracking Transparency, the advertising identifier may be used by Google to measure and personalize ads across other companies' apps and websites. If you decline, no tracking identifier is used and only non-personalized ads are requested. We never sell this data.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may also report crash, performance and other diagnostic data to Google while it serves or measures ads. That diagnostic data is not linked to your identity and is not used to track you. Each app's privacy manifest and App Store privacy answers list exactly which of those data types its own ad integration uses. PopCritters additionally reads performance and crash or hang summaries from Apple's MetricKit; they stay on your device and are never sent to a PopCritters server.

You can review or change your ad consent at any time inside the app. In TabCaddy: Settings → Privacy & Advertising → Ad Privacy Options. In PopCritters: Settings → Privacy & Ads → Ad Privacy Choices. Both entries appear only where your region requires a consent choice, and where required, Google's UMP consent form is shown on first launch and can be reopened from those screens.

For more information about how Google handles this data:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google advertising technologies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Encryption

Private Vault uses AES-GCM encryption via Apple's CryptoKit to encrypt all photos stored in the vault. The encryption key is derived from your passcode and never leaves your device. No one — including us — can access your encrypted photos without your passcode.

Security

Private Vault protects access with Face ID / Touch ID and a passcode lock screen. DayVault also uses Face ID to lock the app and protect your private journal entries. The utility apps (CremaShuffle, HanVault, DiceTrove, Coin Flip, Private Vault, Animal Pop, DayVault) operate strictly offline with no external network transmission.

Children's Privacy

Our apps do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. All content is suitable for general audiences. The ads shown in PopCritters and TabCaddy are not directed at children, and neither app is listed in the Kids category.

Third-Party Services and Data Sharing

Our apps integrate two Google services: Google Mobile Ads, and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent tool in PopCritters and TabCaddy. TabCaddy additionally makes one request of its own — the Venmo profile check described above — and, only when you tap a payment link, hands the recipient's username and the amount per person to Venmo or Cash App (plus your optional note when the link goes to Venmo). We run no analytics of our own, we never sell personal data, and we share nothing with any other third party.
