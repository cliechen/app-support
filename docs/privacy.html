Data Collection

Except for advertising services described below, our apps are designed to operate locally on your device:

  • No analytics or user-tracking accounts are created by developer
  • No personal information (such as name, email, or contacts) is requested or collected by developer
  • Core calculation, game logic, and vault data execute entirely on-device

Local Data Storage

The following app data is stored locally on your device only, using iOS UserDefaults or the app's document directory:

  • TabCaddy: User preferences, tip percentages, custom diner names, and settlement drafts (stored locally in UserDefaults using reason CA92.1)
  • CremaShuffle: Best score, unlocked achievements, effect settings, music/SFX preferences, and tutorial status (stored locally in UserDefaults)
  • HanVault: Learning progress, SRS review records, bookmarked words, daily mission state, and study statistics (stored locally in UserDefaults)
  • DiceTrove: Roll history (stored locally)
  • Coin Flip: Flip history and statistics (stored locally)
  • Private Vault: Encrypted photos and passcode (stored locally, never transmitted)
  • Animal Pop: Game progress and level data (stored locally)
  • DayVault: Calendar events, journal entries, custom event types, and period cycle data (stored locally in document directory)

All locally stored data can be cleared by deleting the app from your device.

Advertising & Third-Party Services (TabCaddy)

While most of our apps are completely offline and ad-free, TabCaddy is a free app supported by third-party advertising via Google AdMob:

  • Google AdMob & UMP: We display banner and occasional interstitial ads using the official Google Mobile Ads SDK. Google may collect anonymous device identifiers, advertising identifiers (IDFA, only if explicitly permitted via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt), and coarse diagnostic metrics to serve non-personalized or personalized ads in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
  • App Tracking Transparency (ATT): TabCaddy asks for your explicit permission before any tracking identifiers are requested. If you decline, only non-personalized ads are shown.
  • 100% Free: TabCaddy is completely free to use with zero in-app purchases, subscriptions, or hidden charges.

Encryption

Private Vault uses AES-GCM encryption via Apple's CryptoKit to encrypt all photos stored in the vault. The encryption key is derived from your passcode and never leaves your device. No one — including us — can access your encrypted photos without your passcode.

Security

For our standalone utility apps (CremaShuffle, HanVault, DiceTrove, Coin Flip, Private Vault, Animal Pop, DayVault), all operations are strictly offline with zero external network transmission. TabCaddy requires network connectivity solely to request and render third-party advertisements via Google AdMob, while its core bill-calculation engine remains entirely local on your device. Private Vault additionally protects access with Face ID / Touch ID and a passcode lock screen. DayVault also uses Face ID to lock the app and protect your private journal entries.

Children's Privacy

Our apps do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. All content is suitable for general audiences.

Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

Except for Google AdMob in TabCaddy (as detailed in the Advertising section above), none of our apps integrate third-party SDKs, analytics tracking, or external APIs. We do not sell or share your personal data with any third parties.
